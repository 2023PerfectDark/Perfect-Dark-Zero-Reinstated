Perfect Dark Zero: Reinstated 1.8 - v126
October 28, 2026. Release-note correction; game build v124.

v126: Updated these in-game release notes in the installer and update download.

v125: US Rev 1 ISO support is shown in Setup. Disc DLC installs automatically, the separate DLC page is skipped, and external Title Update 3 is blocked. Detection uses disc contents, not box branding. Original US discs remain supported with optional TU3 and DLC.

v124: Fixed multiple highlighted gamertags in the lobby and Connected Players. OK and Cancel dialogs accept mouse clicks, and menu hover/click input works after closing them. These fixes were confirmed in game.

Release includes Surface and an optional completed-playthrough shader cache. Choose the recommended cache or build your own. Shader compilation and stutter may still occur on other systems.

Recent features include Extended Options for cutscene subtitles, a mission timer with milliseconds, Manual Reload, keyboard/mouse rebinding and walking speed. Mouse-circle lockpicking and KSI-74 bayonet recovery were confirmed working.

Weather includes snow and cold breath in Missions 4, 6 and 7, plus sparse cave drips in Mission 6. Added cave splashes were disabled after they interfered with weapon effects.

Known limits: random bot head/body buttons remain unresolved. Rev 1 passed installer checks and a DLC map gameplay check; all missions and maps have not been exhaustively tested. This release does not guarantee freedom from crashes or shader stutter.

Updates preserve personal saves and settings. Release downloads and source backup: github.com/2023PerfectDark/Perfect-Dark-Zero-Reinstated
